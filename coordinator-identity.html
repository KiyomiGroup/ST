Why we verify

You are the person who will meet and register Local Taskers, so we check that you are who you say you are before your coordinator account is switched on.

What you need

  • A valid government-issued ID.
  • A phone or computer with a camera.
  • A few minutes in a well-lit place.

What you agree to

Who is responsible. StreetTasker decides why and how your verification information is used. Didit, our identity verification provider, carries out the check on our behalf and under our instructions.

What is collected. A photo of your government-issued ID, including the details printed on it; a short live selfie check to show a real person is present; and a comparison of your face with the photo on your ID. This comparison uses biometric data, which is sensitive personal data.

Why we use it. Only to confirm that you are the person on the ID, so that your coordinator account can be switched on and to help prevent fraud and impersonation. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it.

Who handles it. Didit collects and processes your ID photo, selfie and face comparison, and may do so outside Nigeria. StreetTasker receives the result of the check (for example verified or not completed) and the dates. We do not receive your ID images and we do not ask you to type your ID number into StreetTasker. If a check needs a manual review, authorised StreetTasker staff may view the verification record in Didit. We do not share your verification information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept. We keep the result and dates while your account is active. Didit keeps your images only for a limited period set in our Didit account, and then deletes them.

Your choice and your rights. Giving consent is your choice. The identity check is required to work as a coordinator, and without your consent we cannot verify you. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing support@streettasker.com. Withdrawing does not undo what has already happened. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing the same address, and you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes. If we change this in a way that matters, we will ask for your consent again before you verify.

Your consent is recorded.
