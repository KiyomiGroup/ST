## 2. OWNER DECISIONS (locked; do not re-ask)

### Chat (owner rule 2026-11-05)
A tasker and customer may chat ONLY in a job room, ONLY in these cases:
1. Customer posts a task, tasker makes an offer, customer ACCEPTS it. A customer can have only ONE accepted offer per task at a time; to accept another they must first CANCEL the accepted one.
2. Customer books a service, the tasker ACCEPTS the booking, then they can chat.
Nothing else: a tasker never messages a customer first; no direct messages from profiles; no chat on a pending offer or pending booking.
- Existing chats that break the rule: LEAVE THEM. There is no real chat data on the site yet, it is all test data.
- After a job is completed, or a booking or offer is cancelled: the chat is CLOSED (both completed and cancelled).
- "Cancel an accepted offer" does not exist yet: BUILD IT (stages M3 and M5).
- Done already (2026-11-05): removed the "Have a proposal for [name]? Message" card, the "Contact Client" hero button and the mobile "Message" bar button from `client-profile.html`, `js/client-profile.js`, `css/profile-pages.css`.

### Reviews
- Review only after `bookings.status='completed'` AND `payment_status='released'` (tasker types the release code: `job_submit_code` -> `_release_escrow`). Customer reviews tasker, tasker reviews customer, one each per booking. Visible immediately. Disputes happen before the release code, so every completed/released booking is reviewable.
- Prompts: tasker right after "Code accepted. Payment released to your wallet." (`js/messages-work.js`, `submitCompletionCode(...).then(...)`); customer when the release code is accepted and the task closes, and from the "Please leave a review" notification.
- Rating shown in front of the name everywhere and on everything the person posts (tasks, services). Full review text only on profile pages.
- Local Taskers are normal taskers acted for by the coordinator (real `taskers` row plus placeholder auth user `local_tasker_profiles.tasker_user_id`). Customers can review them; the coordinator writes the Local Tasker's review of the customer through its own DB function.
- Locked once submitted (no edit/delete) is the default, NOT yet confirmed by the owner.

### Homepages and referrals (locked decisions from the homepage handoff, unchanged)
- Successful registration = account created AND email verified. 5 points per successful referral, once per referred account. First-touch attribution, 30-day window (configurable). Random non-sequential codes (`ST` + 6 random alphanumerics), backfilled for existing users. Soft fraud flag above 20 referrals/day (flag, not block).
- Dual-role users: separate referral code, link and points programme per account role. One referral engine, entries keyed by account/programme.
- Tasker rewards: cumulative points never deducted; milestones 100, 200, 400, 800, 1,600... (nth = 100 x 2^(n-1)); each crossed milestone creates ONE saved gift of 30 days Starter; gifts never expire; accept blocked in the BACKEND while any paid plan or active reward month exists (message: "You can activate this reward once your current plan has ended."); one gift active at a time; accepting uses the EXISTING subscription system, no payment; buying a paid plan during a reward month ends the reward (warn first).
- Customer rewards: one gift card wallet balance (no multiple cards, no claim form). PENDING owner confirmation before M17: conversion rate and rounding (owner said "N200 per 1,000 points"), automatic credit or first acceptance, whether a wallet already exists (the repo has `gift-cards.html`, `wallet.html`, `js/wallet-*.js`: check), what it can be spent on.
- Homepage defaults: nearby radius start 10 km, +10 km steps, stop at 8 results, max 100 km (all configurable); 12 cards per row before View All; Featured Taskers cap 20 per tier row, hourly reshuffle among comparable scores; paid tiers need a verified tasker with a complete profile; no selected location = "Choose a location" prompt only; Explore Services links to the existing tasker browse page filtered by category.
- Plan mapping: Business -> Premium, Professional -> Pro, Starter -> Starter, Free -> Standard.
