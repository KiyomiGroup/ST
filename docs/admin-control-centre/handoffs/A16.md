6. **The streams** (never added together; there is no total anywhere in the SQL or the page):
   - **Regular Tasker commission**: `platform_fee` of the released escrow row, counted on the day the release code was entered (A15b's rule).
   - **Local job commission (platform share)**: the platform share of the fee on a Local job, on the day of the final release. **This is a fifth box; the spec listed four.** I kept it apart because Local Tasker jobs must not be inside "Regular Tasker commission" (plan test) and the platform share is still platform income.
   - **Subscription revenue**: `subscription_receipts` with a paid time, `amount_kobo` as stored.
   - **Coordinator plan revenue**: `payment_intents` purpose `tier_upgrade`, status `paid`.
   - **Withdrawal fees**: `withdrawals.fee` on `success` rows, as stored. Paystack's own cost is not subtracted. This is my reading of "other platform income".
   - **Memo, not platform income**: the coordinator's share of Local fees.
   - **Held, not earned yet**: `platform_fee` of escrow rows still `held`.
   - **Not counted**: gift-card fees (no fee is stored anywhere), refunds recorded by support (not subtracted), `test_archive_*` tables (never read). Rates are never recomputed (`ARCHITECTURE.md` B5 item 1).
