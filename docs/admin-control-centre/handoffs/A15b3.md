4. **Settlement matching.** The accountant types each settlement with the existing label "Paystack settlement paid into the bank". Estimate = Paystack-money labels (`customer_payment_job`, `wallet_topup`, `gift_card_sold`, `donation_received`, `other_paystack_payment`) from the start of the previous settlement's day (Nigeria time) up to the start of this settlement's day, minus typed `paystack_fee_collection` lines in the same window. Matched = within 50 naira. Also an estimate for "not yet received". Always labelled an estimate.
5. **Cash position.** Balances of the client bank account, operating bank account and Paystack Balance are TYPED (with the date read), never computed. Owed = wallet balance + pending (A17 columns) plus Local payouts queued or processing. Safe to take back = cash held minus owed; it does NOT subtract gift card balances (columns unseen), and the page says so. Not available until all three balances exist.
6. **Capital guard.** A repayment larger than the capital outstanding is refused by the database; reversing a capital_in that was already partly repaid is refused. Reversed lines are not counted.
