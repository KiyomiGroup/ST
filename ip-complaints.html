1. Our approach

1.1

We respect intellectual property and expect users to do the same. This process lets a rights holder report content on StreetTasker that infringes copyright, trademark or other rights under Nigerian law. It is not a US DMCA process and we do not claim DMCA safe-harbour status.

2. How to report

Send the following to admin@streettasker.com:

  • Your name, contact details, and your relationship to the work.
  • description of the work and proof of ownership or authority.
  • e exact web address (URL) or location of the allegedly infringing content.
  • statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised, and that your information is accurate.
  • ur signature (typed is acceptable).

3. What we do

3.1

We review the notice, may remove or disable access to the content, and tell the user who posted it. They may send a response explaining why they believe the content is lawful, and we consider it.

3.2

We keep records of notices and responses. Users who repeatedly infringe may have their accounts suspended or terminated.

3.3

False or abusive notices may lead to action against the sender. You keep your right to go to court.
