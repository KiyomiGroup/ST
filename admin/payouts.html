Earnings and payouts

A read-only watch on money going out to taskers. It shows what is waiting, stuck or failed. It never sends, retries, refunds or changes anything, and it never calls Paystack. Reconciliation with Paystack runs from the withdrawal-reconcile function, not from this page. Amounts are in naira.
