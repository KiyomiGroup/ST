Identity verification

A read-only view of the identity checks done by the provider (Didit). It shows results only: the status, three pass or fail checks, the NIN match word and the reason codes. It never shows ID numbers, photos or dates of birth, and it has no button that marks anyone verified. A person becomes verified only when the provider says so. Membership (PRO or PREMIUM) is a different thing and is not shown here.
