Subscriptions

Who is on which plan, what was paid, and which payments need a person to look. Read only: nothing here changes a plan, refunds, credits or cancels, and the page never calls Paystack. A plan does not renew by itself; a person continues it by paying again, so an expired plan is called ended. Coordinator plans and Local Taskers are not subscriptions and are not here. Amounts are in naira.
