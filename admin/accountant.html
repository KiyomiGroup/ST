Accountant

The daily amount to send to Paystack, the profit, and the ledger of every money step. This page only shows and records. It never moves money, never calls Paystack and never changes a wallet, escrow row or payment.

Send to Paystack

Each day at 9:00 pm Nigeria time the site adds up the tasker money of jobs whose release code was entered since 9:00 pm the day before. That is the amount to put in Paystack for taskers within 24 hours. Commission is the platform’s own money and is never part of it. From site records.

Daily reports

Newest first. A day stays “To send” until someone with permission records that the money was sent.

Cash position

The balances below are typed in by the accountant from the bank and the Paystack dashboard, with the date he read them. The site cannot see a real balance. “Owed” comes from site records.

Record a balance

Settlements

Record each Paystack settlement under “Record a money step” (Paystack settlement paid into the bank). Each one is compared with an estimate: payments confirmed since the previous settlement’s day, minus the Paystack collection fees typed for the same days. The estimate is not Paystack’s own figure; “matched” means within ₦50.

Owner capital

Money the owner put in is a loan to the business, not income. All time, from typed entries.

Record a money step

Fees from the Paystack statement, expenses, settlements and capital. A new line is added to the ledger. A line is never edited or deleted; a mistake is fixed with a reversal. Amounts are in naira.

At least 5 characters.

Ledger

Every labelled money step. “Site” lines are copied from the site’s own records; “Typed” lines were entered by a person. Reversed lines are crossed out in the totals.
