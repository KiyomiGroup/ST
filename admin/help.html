Help

Short notes for StreetTasker admins. Nothing on this page is live data.

Signing in and two-step verification

  • Sign in at /admin/login.html with an account that has admin access. Use a login made only for admin work.
  • Set up your authenticator app on My profile. Do this before two-step is switched on for everyone.
  • When two-step is required, you enter the 6-digit code after your password. Codes change every 30 seconds.
  • Never share a code, the set-up key or your password. Nobody at StreetTasker will ask for them.
  • Lost your phone? Tell the owner or another Super Admin straight away. They can reset your two-step from the database.
  • Use Sign out on shared computers. It also signs you out of the rest of the StreetTasker site on that device.

What you can see and do

  • The menu only shows pages your role allows. If a page you need is missing, ask a Super Admin to add the permission.
  • Every action is checked on the server again and written to the activity log. Hiding a button is not what keeps data safe.
  • Sensitive changes need a second person to approve them. You cannot approve your own request.
  • Private details such as phone numbers and addresses are hidden until you choose to reveal them, and each reveal is logged.

What admin never does

  • It never moves money, calls the payment provider, or changes a wallet, escrow or payment status. Refund and adjustment screens only record what was decided.
  • It never sets or lowers a Local Tasker's price, and never skips a worker's agreement to a job.
  • It never marks anyone as identity verified. PRO and PREMIUM are membership labels, not identity.
  • It never deletes history. Removing someone keeps their records.

When something looks wrong

  • Stop and do not retry a money-related action. Tell the owner (the Super Admin) what you saw and what you clicked.
  • A page that says “Something went wrong” changed nothing. Use Retry once, then report it.
  • If you think someone else has your password or code, change your password on My profile, use Sign out of all devices, and tell the owner.

The build notes for the admin area (plan, database changes, stage handoffs) are kept in the project files by the owner. They are not part of this website.
