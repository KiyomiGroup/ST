      Frequently asked questions

      When do I earn points?

      When a friend you invited has signed up and verified their email. Points are never taken away.

      How does my friend get their 5 points?

      They sign up with your link, or type your referral code in the optional box on the sign-up page. The points appear in their own referral rewards.

      Do clicks on my link earn anything?

      No. Only people who sign up count.

      Can I use my own code?

      No. A code only works for someone else’s new account.
