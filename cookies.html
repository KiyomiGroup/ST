1. What this policy covers

1.1

This policy explains the cookies, browser storage and similar technologies StreetTasker uses. It reflects what the site currently uses.

1.2

We use no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics tools.

3. Your choices

3.1

You can clear storage, block cookies or turn off notifications in your browser settings. Blocking essential items may stop sign-in and payments from working.

3.2

If we add optional analytics, advertising or similar tools, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first, through a banner that matches the real behaviour of the site.
