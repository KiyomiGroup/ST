            On the day

              Release the payment

              The customer sees this code in their Job Room once they are happy with the work. Never ask for it before the job is done.

              A wrong code uses one of three attempts. After three wrong codes the booking is locked and only a StreetTasker administrator can unlock it. Entering the right code releases the payment once. It can’t be undone.
