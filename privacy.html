4. Messages and automated monitoring

4.1

Messages sent through StreetTasker are scanned by automated systems to detect sharing of off-platform contact details, and flagged messages are reviewed by authorised staff. This helps us enforce the Terms and protect users. Automated flags do not by themselves permanently end an account; staff review repeated violations.

6. International transfers

6.1

Some providers (for example Didit, Supabase, Resend and messaging providers) may process data outside Nigeria. We transfer personal data abroad only where the NDPA allows, using an adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards (such as contract terms) or your consent where needed.

7. How long we keep your data

7.1

We keep your personal data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data.

7.2

After deletion we keep only what the law requires us to keep (for example payment, wallet and tax records) or what we need to deal with an open dispute, fraud investigation or legal claim, and only for as long as that purpose lasts. Copies in backups are removed when the backups are overwritten in the normal way.

7.3

Identity verification images are held by Didit, not by us, for the limited period set in our Didit account and are then deleted. See the Verification Consent Notice.

7.4

To delete your account, use your account settings or email support@streettasker.com.

8. Your rights

8.1

Under the NDPA you may ask to: be informed about our processing; access your data; correct it; erase it where applicable; restrict or object to processing; receive it in a portable format where applicable; withdraw consent at any time; and not be subject to a decision based only on automated processing that significantly affects you.

8.2

To make a request, email support@streettasker.com or support@streettasker.com. We may verify your identity first, and will reply without undue delay and within the period the law sets. We may refuse or limit a request where the law allows (for example, records we must keep).

8.3

Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already done. If you withdraw consent to verification, some features will not be available.

8.4

You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (https://ndpc.gov.ng).

9. Security

9.1

We use measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication, role-based access, and restricted staff access. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong unique password and never share codes or one-time passwords.

9.2

If a personal data breach occurs we will assess it and notify the NDPC and affected people as the law requires.

10. Children

The Platform is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will act on it.

11. Gift cards

When you buy a gift card we collect the recipient's name and email address or phone number, your name as sender, your optional message, the delivery date, the card code and its transactions, and the network address of the purchase request to limit abuse. We use this only to take payment, deliver the gift, let the recipient redeem and use it, and prevent fraud. We share what is needed with Paystack (payments), our email provider (emails) and, for a gift sent to a phone number, our SMS provider and WhatsApp. Recipient details are used only to deliver that gift. Corporate gifting enquiries are used only to reply.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about significant changes by email or in-app notice, and show the effective date at the top.
