1. Our commitment

1.1

We want StreetTasker to be usable by as many people as possible and we do not tolerate unlawful discrimination.

1.2

The Platform is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers, uses a standard readable typeface, and gives users in-app and email routes to get help. We have not had the Platform independently audited for accessibility, and we do not claim conformance with any accessibility standard.

2. Reporting a barrier

2.1

If you find something hard to use, email support@streettasker.com with the page and a description. We will reply and look for a reasonable solution or alternative way to help.

2.2

You can ask for reasonable accommodation, such as help to post a task or complete a booking.
