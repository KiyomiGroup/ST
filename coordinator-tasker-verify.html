Start the check

Read or explain this to the Local Tasker first. It is the same notice customers and taskers see.

Who is responsible. StreetTasker decides why and how the verification information is used. Didit, our identity verification provider, carries out the check on our behalf and under our instructions.

What is collected. A photo of a government-issued ID, including the details printed on it; a short live selfie check to show a real person is present; and a comparison of the face with the photo on the ID. This comparison uses biometric data, which is sensitive personal data.

Why it is used. Only to confirm that the person is the person on the ID, so that people on StreetTasker can trust who they are dealing with and to help prevent fraud and impersonation. It is not used for marketing and it is not sold.

Who handles it. Didit collects and processes the ID photo, selfie and face comparison, and may do so outside Nigeria. StreetTasker receives the result of the check (for example verified or not completed) and the dates, not the ID images, and does not ask for an ID number. If a check needs a manual review, authorised StreetTasker staff may view the verification record in Didit. The information is not shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept. StreetTasker keeps the result and dates while the account is active. Didit keeps the images only for a limited period set in our Didit account, and then deletes them.

Their choice and rights. Giving consent is their choice. Verification is required to be offered on StreetTasker, and without consent we cannot verify them. They can withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@streettasker.com. Withdrawing does not undo what has already happened. They can ask to see, correct or delete their information by emailing the same address, and can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Your part as coordinator. You explain this to them first, and you record that they agreed, how, and when. The check is done on your phone, with them beside you.
