How visibility works

Three levels, from everyone to the plans that include more.

Standard search placement

Every tasker, on every plan, appears in search. Results are ordered by how well they match what the customer is looking for.

Featured placement

On plans that include it, your services can appear in featured spots. Eligible taskers take turns, so spots rotate rather than going to one person.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you choose.

Can I start with a free plan?

Yes. Every tasker can start on the Free plan at no cost and with no card. You get a monthly offer allowance and a service listing allowance, so you can set up your profile and start replying to tasks straight away. Upgrade only when you want more.

What happens when my offers run out?

You cannot send new offers until your allowance renews or you move to a plan with a bigger one. Free allowances renew at the start of each calendar month; paid allowances start again with each period you pay for. Offers you already sent stay as they are, and you can still edit them. Withdrawing an offer does not give it back to your allowance. Your bookings are not limited by your plan.

Can I change my subscription?

Yes. When you upgrade, the unused time on your current plan is credited toward the new one, and Paystack shows you the exact amount before you pay. To move to a lower plan, choose it when your current plan has ended. Until then you keep everything you paid for.

What happens to my listings if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. If you have more listings than the new plan allows, we tell you before anything changes and you choose which ones stay active. Listings you do not choose are paused only after the date we gave you: your most recently updated listings stay active up to the new limit, and the rest are paused. A paused listing is kept, and you can reactivate it whenever you have room.

Does featured placement guarantee bookings?

No. Featured placement gives your services more exposure, and featured spots rotate fairly between eligible taskers. Customers choose who to hire, so no plan guarantees bookings, customers or income.

Can I cancel my subscription?

There is nothing to cancel. Your plan never renews by itself, and we never keep your card or bank details. You keep your plan’s benefits until the end of the period you have paid for, then you move to the Free plan. The listing rules above apply if you have more listings than Free allows.

How does monthly renewal work?

Subscriptions are paid with Paystack only, one month at a time. Your plan does not renew automatically and we do not save your card or bank details: Paystack handles every payment. To continue after your plan ends, you pay again with Paystack. If you do not, you move to the Free plan. Wallet balance cannot be used to pay for a subscription.

Is identity verification included?

Identity verification is not a paid feature. Every StreetTasker user completes it, whatever their plan, and a membership label never means that someone is verified.

What is the difference between PRO and PREMIUM membership?

Both are membership labels shown after a tasker’s name on profiles, search results and offers, so customers can see which paid plan someone is on. PREMIUM is the higher level and goes with the most features; the plan comparison on this page shows exactly what each plan includes. A label shows a plan only. It is not a check, a rating or a promise of anything.
