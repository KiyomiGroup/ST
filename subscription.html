How visibility works

Three levels, from everyone to the plans that include more.

Standard search placement

Every tasker, on every plan, appears in search. Results are ordered by how well they match what the customer is looking for.

Featured placement

On plans that include it, your services can appear in featured spots. Eligible taskers take turns, so spots rotate rather than going to one person.

Premium recommendations

On the plan that includes it, you can be recommended ahead of others when your services are a good match for the customer.

No plan guarantees bookings, customers or income. Visibility helps customers find you; hiring is always the customer’s choice. Plans never override relevance or safety checks.
