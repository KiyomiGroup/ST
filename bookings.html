How You Get Paid

  1. Confirm the requestThe customer gets a payment request in the job room straight away.
  2. Wait for escrowThe customer pays in the chat. Only start work once the booking shows Held in Escrow.
  3. Mark the job as doneDo this in the job room. The customer reviews the work there.
  4. Enter the codeThe customer gives you a 6-digit code. Enter it in the job room and your payout is released.

Escrow Protection

  • Customer funds are locked in escrow before you start.
  • Your payout is released when you enter the customer’s code.
  • A 12% platform fee is taken from each job’s value.
