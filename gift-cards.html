Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Do StreetTasker gift cards expire?

No. StreetTasker gift cards never expire, and there are no inactivity fees or hidden deductions. The full balance stays on the card until it is used.

Can a Cleaning Gift Card be used for other services if budget remains?

Yes. Category cards are priced around average task costs for that kind of work, but they are a guide, not a limit. Any balance left after a booking stays on the card and can be used on any other task on StreetTasker.

How does escrow protection apply when paying with a gift card?

A task paid with a gift card is protected the same way as any other StreetTasker booking. The payment is held in escrow and only released to the Tasker once the work is done. Any unused balance stays on the card.

How do I check my card balance or top up?

Enter your voucher code in the balance checker above to see what is left on your card. To add more value, you can buy another gift card from the cards above.
