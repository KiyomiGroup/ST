Tips to Win More Tasks

  1. Reply fastApplications sent within 15 minutes of a task being posted are far more likely to be noticed.
  2. Itemise your offerSeparating parts from labour builds confidence and cuts back-and-forth haggling.
  3. Mention your areaNaming your neighbourhood reassures the client you can arrive quickly.

Escrow Dispute Protection

Never accept offline cash deposits. When a client accepts your offer, their payment is held in escrow before you start, and released when the job is completed.
