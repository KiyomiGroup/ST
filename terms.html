11. Payments, commission, clearing, wallet and withdrawals

This clause is a summary. The Payment, Commission and Wallet Policy gives the full rules and worked examples, and forms part of these Terms.

Payment through the Platform

11.1

Customers must pay the Task Amount through the Platform for engagements covered by the Platform payment facility. Payments are processed by Paystack or another authorised payment provider, and the provider's own terms also apply.

11.2

Paying or arranging payment outside the Platform for work found through it is not allowed.

11.3

A payment notification does not always mean funds have finally settled or are available for withdrawal.

Commission and processing fees

11.4

We keep a Platform Commission of 12% of the Task Amount on engagements that are completed and confirmed, subject to refunds, reversals and adjustments. The Tasker's entitlement is the remaining 88%, before Processing Fees and other disclosed deductions.

11.5

Processing Fees charged by Paystack or another provider are borne by the Tasker and deducted from the Tasker's earnings in addition to the 12% commission. The fee or the way it is calculated is disclosed before the engagement is accepted or payment is processed. We will not invent, retrospectively increase or impose any processing charge.

11.6

We will not change the commission on an engagement already accepted by both parties. Any future change will be communicated before it applies to new engagements.

11.7

Example: on a ₦100,000 task, the commission is ₦12,000 and the Tasker's entitlement before the Processing Fee is ₦88,000. The Paystack Processing Fee is then deducted. At Paystack's current local rate of 1.5% + ₦100 this is ₦1,600, leaving ₦86,400 (see the Payment Policy for the rate source and exclusions).

Completion Code and clearing

11.8

After the work is done, the Tasker asks the Customer for the Completion Code. A valid code accepted by the Platform ordinarily confirms that the Customer authorises completion.

11.9

If a Customer will not or cannot give the code despite satisfactory completion, the Tasker may ask for a manual completion review. We may review evidence before releasing payment without the code.

11.10

After a valid code, the Tasker's earnings enter a 24-business-hour Clearing Period, shown as pending. The Clearing Period allows reconciliation, payment confirmation and the identification of disputes or irregularities. Business hours run Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm West Africa Time. Saturdays, Sundays and Nigerian public holidays are not counted. Clearing does not guarantee immediate bank settlement.

Wallet and withdrawals

11.11

We keep a Wallet showing each Tasker's pending and available balances and history. The Wallet is a platform ledger and balance feature. A displayed balance does not by itself mean the money is held in a separate bank account, trust or escrow for you.

11.12

After clearing, eligible earnings move to your available balance. You must request withdrawals manually. The minimum withdrawal is currently ₦500, to a verified bank account in your own name.

11.13

Withdrawals may be delayed or held because of provider or bank issues, suspected fraud, unresolved disputes, verification checks or legal requirements. A withdrawal request does not guarantee instant receipt.

11.14

If a payout fails or is reversed, the amount is returned to your available Wallet balance after reconciliation.

Holds, reversals and adjustments

11.15

We may place a proportionate hold on the affected amount, with notice, while we review a genuine dispute, suspected fraud, chargeback, unauthorised transaction, security investigation or legal restriction.

11.16

We may correct genuine accounting errors, duplicate credits, fraudulent transactions and authorised reversals. If a payment is reversed or charged back after earnings were credited or withdrawn, we may recover the amount properly attributable to it, using fair procedures and supporting records. You will not be held responsible for adjustments that lack a reasonable basis and supporting records.

11.17

You may ask us to explain any account transaction by contacting support. We keep records of payments, commissions, fees, wallet entries, clearing and withdrawals.

14. Gift cards

14.1

What a gift card is. A StreetTasker gift card is a prepaid voucher for services booked through the Platform. It is not a bank account, deposit or electronic money. It cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for cash or sent to a bank account, and it earns no interest.

14.2

Buying. You need an account to buy a gift card. The price is the face value in naira with no extra fee. Payment is taken by Paystack and the card is issued only after Paystack confirms full payment. You are responsible for entering the recipient's email address or phone number correctly, and we are not responsible for a gift sent to a wrong address or number you supplied.

14.3

Delivery. We email the gift, send it by text message or WhatsApp to a phone number, or provide a printable card, depending on your choice. Gifts can be scheduled for a later date. If a gift does not arrive, contact us and we can send it again.

14.4

Redeeming. Anyone holding a card's code can redeem it, so keep the code private. Once redeemed, the card is added to that person's Wallet, belongs to that account, and cannot be moved to another account.

14.5

Using a card. A card pays for bookings made through the Platform. A category card works only for bookings in its category (for example cleaning); a universal card works for any booking. If a booking costs more than the balance, the balance is used first and the rest is paid another way. Gift cards do not expire and have no inactivity fees.

14.6

Cancelled bookings. If a booking paid with a gift card is cancelled, the gift card part is returned to the same card, not as cash. Any part paid by card or transfer follows the refund process.

14.7

Refunds. An unredeemed gift card can be refunded to the original payment method if you ask within 7 days of buying it. After that, or once a card is redeemed or partly used, it is not refundable, without affecting any statutory right.

14.8

Lost or stolen cards. Contact us with your order reference as soon as possible. If the card is unredeemed we can cancel it and issue a replacement. We cannot recover value from a card already redeemed by someone else.

14.9

Misuse. We may cancel a gift card and its balance if it was obtained by fraud, paid for by a payment that was reversed or charged back, or used in breach of these Terms.

14.10

Gift card questions: support@streettasker.com. Corporate gifting enquiries are used only to reply to the enquiry.
