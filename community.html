1. Our standards

1.1

StreetTasker only works if people can trust each other. Everyone must act honestly, lawfully and respectfully.

1.2

Taskers and Customers must not harass, threaten, abuse or discriminate (including by gender, religion, tribe, disability or any protected characteristic), commit or encourage violence, steal, defraud, impersonate, misuse personal data, enter unsafe premises or push others to do so.

2. Prohibited tasks

2.1

You may not post or accept tasks that are illegal; dangerous; exploitative; sexual or adult services; involve weapons, drugs or other controlled items; harm or mistreat people or animals; cheat in exams or forge documents; require a licence you do not hold; or otherwise break Nigerian law. Some trades need a licence or insurance by law, and Taskers must hold what the law requires for their work.

2.2

Do not pay or take payment outside the Platform for work found here, and do not share off-platform contact details (see Terms clause 8).

3. Safety guidance

  • Never pay large sums before work begins and only pay through StreetTasker.
  • r first meetings, choose public or shared places where possible, and tell someone where you are.
  • eck the Tasker's profile, reviews and verification status.
  • ver share bank PINs, passwords or one-time codes with anyone, including people claiming to be StreetTasker.
  • ve your Completion Code only after the work is complete.

In an emergency, contact the police or emergency services first. StreetTasker cannot respond to emergencies.

4. Reporting and enforcement

4.1

Report a user, task, message or listing using the in-app report feature or email support@streettasker.com. Keep screenshots, messages and payment records as evidence.

4.2

We may remove content, warn, restrict messaging, hold payments in proportion to the issue, suspend or permanently remove accounts. Repeat or serious offenders may be removed permanently. Where law requires, we may report matters to the authorities.

4.3

If you are restricted you can appeal to hello@streettasker.com with your reasons and evidence.
